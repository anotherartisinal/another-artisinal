An object must be made in-house.

A principle 1

An object must be biodegradable.

A principle 2

An object must be designed for disassembly and alterations.

A principle 3

An object must be designed for steam wash and hand wash.

A principle 4

Synthetic or semi-synthetic fibers and material are strictly forbidden.

A principle 5

Exotic leather and fur is strictly prohibited.

A principle 6

Solid elements must be detachable.

A principle 7

Hand stitching techniques are preferable.

A principle 8

Printing techniques should be avoided.

A principle 9

Production waste must be submitted for recycling.

A principle 10

A deadstock object must be remanufactured or repurposed for recommerce.

A principle 11

Recycling and disposal instructions must be clear and visible.

A principle 12

Branding on the exterior of an object is forbidden.

A principle 13

A designer, artisans and contributors must remain anonymous.

A principle 14

This manifesto can be implemented to any type of industrial design. Any esthetics and narrative is valid, most of the practices are not.
